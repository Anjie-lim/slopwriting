What is AI Slop?

AI Slop refers to visible rendering artifacts or logical inconsistencies produced by generative AI image models. These errors occur when the model fails to accurately represent anatomy, text, object relationships, counting, or scene logic. While many generated images appear convincing at first glance, closer inspection often reveals subtle mistakes that distinguish them from authentic photographs.

Common Types of AI Slop

The following categories represent the most common AI-generated image artifacts encountered during evaluation.


🟦 Text Rendering

Unreadable words, distorted typography, misspelled signs, malformed OCR.

🟨 Anatomy

Extra fingers, merged limbs, distorted faces, impossible body structures.

🟪 Object Integrity

Merged objects, broken geometry, unrealistic object construction.

🟥 Scene Logic

Objects violating physics or appearing in impossible locations.

🟩 Counting

Incorrect numbers of people, animals, fingers, chairs, or objects.

Key Takeaways

  • AI-generated images often appear realistic from a distance but reveal artifacts during close inspection.
  • Text rendering remains one of the easiest AI tells to detect.
  • Hands, faces, object interactions, and counting should always be examined carefully.
  • Systematic inspection is more reliable than relying on intuition alone.

Prompt Building Components

High-value prompts often combine three or four complementary components that challenge different aspects of an AI image model. Rather than trying to include every possible element, choose the components that best support the type of artifact you want to expose. Different combinations can reveal different types of AI tells.
